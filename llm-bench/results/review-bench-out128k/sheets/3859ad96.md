## Findings

### [MINOR] Retry protocol always says "the existing record" where no record may exist yet
**Where:** NF4, doc revision 5, lines 243-246 ("Herpoging na EEXIST").
**Claim:** Every retry branch updates "het bestaande record" (identical to skipped_identical; different to dest_path update).
**Reality:** For a route C first-attempt EEXIST at step 2 (line 237: "er is dan nog geen eigen bestand" — and step 3's record insert has not happened yet) and for a direct F16 identical-candidate skip (line 131), no record exists at the moment of the outcome.
**Consequence:** An implementer following NF4 literally could emit no record at all for a first-collision identical skip, violating F19b's one-record-per-file intent. F19b line 143 ("Bestaat er nog geen record, dan wordt er direct een record met dat resultaat ingevoegd") actually covers both cases, so behavior is spec'd correctly overall.
**Fix:** wording only — in the retry bullets, refer to F19b step 1 (upsert: insert when absent, update when present).

### [MINOR] Date preservation stated for copy only in F15
**Where:** F15, line 126 ("Bij kopiëren blijven de wijzigings- en de aanmaakdatum van de bron behouden"); AC3a line 271 ("na een kopie").
**Claim:** Metadata preservation is a copy-mode property.
**Reality:** Route B/C also move via copy (line 125), and route B step 4 (line 230) sets both dates for copy and move. In move mode the source is deleted, so the destination's preserved dates are the only remaining record of them — the property matters there at least as much.
**Consequence:** A tester reading F15/AC3a literally would not require date preservation for a move-via-copy, even though NF4 mandates it; risk of an untested regression on the move path.
**Fix:** wording only — extend F15 line 126 and AC3a line 271 to "bij kopiëren en bij verplaatsen via een kopie".
