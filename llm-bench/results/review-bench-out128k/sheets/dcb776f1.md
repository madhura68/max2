#### [MINOR] Herpoging section wording for Route C step 2 EEXIST
**Where:** `sources/doc` lines 236–246 (Route C and Herpoging)
**Claim:** The Herpoging section states: "Anders: `dest_path` in het bestaande record wordt bijgewerkt en de app gaat verder bij … route C stap 2."
**Reality:** In Route C, `O_EXCL` fails at step 2 (line 237), which is *before* the pending record is inserted at step 3. Therefore no record exists yet — there is no "bestaande record" to update. The retry logic would skip the update as a no-op; the intended flow (pick new name, retry step 2) is still correct.
**Consequence:** The wording could confuse an implementer who follows the letter rather than the intent. No actual defect because the counter (max 3) and eventual `failed` result are unambiguous.
**Fix:** In the Herpoging section, clarify: for Route C step 2 EEXIST (no record yet), skip the record-update step; or restructure so step 3 inserts immediately after step 2 succeeds and retries are handled before the insert.
