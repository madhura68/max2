# Transcript 455ec6 (R10: Engels)

### Gebruiker

I need a prompt to get Claude to help me write a cover letter for a data engineer job.

### Model

Natuurlijk, ik help je daar graag mee. Om een prompt te schrijven die echt aansluit op jouw situatie, stel ik vier korte vragen:

1. Welke vacature is het? [standaard: je plakt de vacaturetekst in de prompt, plus eventueel de URL]
2. Wat is jouw achtergrond? [standaard: je plakt je CV of een korte samenvatting van je ervaring in, inclusief technische stack zoals Python, SQL, Spark, Airflow, AWS/GCP]
3. Hoe lang en in welke taal moet de brief zijn? [standaard: 300–400 woorden, in het Engels]
4. Mag Claude ook zelf een toon en opbouw kiezen, of wil je die sturen? [standaard: zelf kiezen, maar wel concreet: geen clichés, geen "I am writing to apply for", wel 2–3 specifieke projecten uit je CV gekoppeld aan de vacature]

### Gebruiker

Go with your suggestions. Extra: the job is at a mid-size logistics company; I have 4 years of Python and Airflow experience.

### Model

```
<context>
I am applying for a data engineer position at a mid-size logistics company. I have 4 years of professional experience with Python and Airflow. The cover letter will be read by a hiring manager who is technical but busy, so it should be concrete and skimmable.
</context>

<material>
[VACATURETEKST: paste the full job description here]
[CV SAMENVATTING: paste your CV or a short summary of your experience here, including projects, tools and achievements]
</material>

<task>
Write a cover letter for this data engineer position.
</task>

<constraints>
- Length: 300–400 words.
- Language: English.
- No clichés. Do not start with "I am writing to apply for" or similar generic openings.
- Connect at least 2–3 specific projects or achievements from my CV to the requirements in the job description.
- Mention my 4 years of Python and Airflow experience naturally, not as a list.
- The company is in logistics; where relevant, show awareness of that domain (e.g. tracking data, route optimization, supply chain data pipelines).
- Tone: professional, confident, concrete. No flattery, no "I am passionate about".
- Do not invent facts, projects or numbers that are not in the material. If a requirement from the job description is not covered by my CV, address it briefly and honestly rather than faking a match.
</constraints>

<done_when>
- Every specific claim about my experience can be traced back to the CV material.
- At least 2–3 CV items are explicitly linked to job description requirements.
- The letter fits 300–400 words.
- No generic opening line.
</done_when>

<output_format>
The cover letter as plain text, ready to paste into an email or application form. No extra commentary before or after the letter.
</output_format>

<check_in>
If a key part of the job description does not match my CV at all, stop and tell me before writing the full letter.
</check_in>
```
