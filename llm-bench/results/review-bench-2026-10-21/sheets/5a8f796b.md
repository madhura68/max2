## Findings

### [MINOR] Route partition does not explicitly cover same-volume copy on a capable volume
**Where:** `sources/doc` line 226 (Route B header); lines 220, 235 (Route A/C headers); F15 lines 123–125.
**Claim:** the three routes partition every copy/move case: A = "verplaatsen binnen hetzelfde volume, mét `RENAME_EXCL`"; B = "kopiëren of verplaatsen tussen volumes, doelvolume mét `RENAME_EXCL`"; C = "doelvolume zonder `RENAME_EXCL`".
**Reality:** a copy whose source and destination are on the *same* volume, with `RENAME_EXCL` present, matches no route header: A is restricted to moving, B reads as "between volumes", C requires the absence of the capability.
**Consequence:** the implementer must infer that same-volume copy also uses the temp + `renamex_np` path (the only sensible reading, and it works identically on one volume), but the routing decision in the safety-critical section should not rely on inference.
**Fix:** repair — one line: broaden the Route B header, e.g. "Route B: kopiëren (ook binnen hetzelfde volume) of verplaatsen tussen volumes, doelvolume mét `RENAME_EXCL`." No behavior change.

### [MINOR] F0d points at NF4 for stop semantics NF4 does not define
**Where:** `sources/doc` line 86 (F0d).
**Claim:** "Stop tijdens `processing` maakt het lopende bestand eerst af, volgens NF4."
**Reality:** NF4 (lines 213–251) defines publication routes and crash cleanup, not Stop/in-flight-file behavior; the stop semantics live in F0d itself (finish the current file, then `cancelled` via F19c, lines 147–149, 151).
**Consequence:** a reader following the reference finds nothing; no functional impact. The AC4a case (line 294) is consistent with F0d's own wording.
**Fix:** repair — point at F19b/F19c, or delete "volgens NF4".

### [MINOR] §1a "keyword zoals ingetypt" vs F14 sanitization — deviation should be declared
**Where:** `sources/doc` line 30 (§1a) vs line 118 (F14).
**Claim:** the binding decision (normative authority per the round brief) says the destination is `<bestemming>/<keyword zoals ingetypt>/<datum van de actie>/`.
**Reality:** F14 strips leading/trailing whitespace and leading dots, collapses internal whitespace, and replaces `/` and `:` with `-`. That is necessary filesystem safety, but literally not "zoals ingetypt".
**Consequence:** a strict reading of §1a as the authority could flag F14 as a deviation from a binding decision, inviting a needless round. AC5 (line 296, `janpeter visser` → `janpeter visser/`) is consistent under both readings.
**Fix:** repair — add one line to F14: the sanitization is a filesystem-safety normalization of §1a's "zoals ingetypt", not a change to the decision. No behavior change.
