Write a compelling cover letter for this Data Engineer role. The letter should bridge my 4 years of Python/Airflow experience with the specific needs of a mid-size logistics company.
</task>

<output_format>
- **Tone:** Professional, pragmatic, and enthusiastic. Avoid overly corporate jargon; focus on practical impact.
- **Structure:**
  1. **Opening:** State the role and briefly mention why logistics/data interests me.
  2. **Technical Fit:** Highlight my 4 years with Python and Airflow. Frame these not just as tools I know, but as solutions to reliability and orchestration problems common in logistics.

</output_format>

<constraints>

- Focus on the *impact* of my work (e.g., "reduced pipeline failure rate by X%") rather than just listing tasks.
</constraints>
