# Transcript 33c01b (R10: Engels)

### Gebruiker

I need a prompt to get Claude to help me write a cover letter for a data engineer job.

### Model

Goed, wat ik nodig heb om een scherp prompt te maken:

1. Heeft u al een cv, job description of beide? [standaard: u voegt beide toe aan de prompt]
2. Hoeveel ervaring heeft u en wat is uw belangrijkste specialisatie (bijv. cloud, data pipelines, streaming)? [standaard: 3+ jaar, focus op cloud data pipelines]
3. Welke tone of stijl: formeel, semi-formeel of conversational? [standaard: semi-formeel, professioneel maar niet stijf]
4. Hoelang mag de cover letter zijn? [standaard: 300–400 woorden, 1 pagina]

### Gebruiker

Go with your suggestions. Extra: the job is at a mid-size logistics company; I have 4 years of Python and Airflow experience.

### Model

```
<context>
I am applying for a data engineer position at a mid-size logistics company. I have 4 years of experience with Python and Apache Airflow. I will attach my CV and the job description as material.
</context>

<material>
[Attach: CV]
[Attach: Job description]
</material>

<task>
Write a cover letter for this data engineer application. Tailor it to the specific responsibilities and technologies listed in the job description, and highlight how my Python and Airflow experience maps to those needs.
</task>

<constraints>
- Semi-formal tone: professional but not stiff.
- 300–400 words, fits on one page.
- Do not invent projects, metrics or employers. Use only facts from my CV.
- If a requirement in the job description is not covered by my CV, address it briefly without overclaiming.
</constraints>

<done_when>
- Every specific claim about my experience is traceable to the attached CV.
- The letter references at least two concrete responsibilities from the job description.
- Word count is between 300 and 400.
</done_when>

<output_format>
The cover letter as plain text, ready to paste into an email or application form. No subject line, no signature block beyond a closing line.
</output_format>
```

Aannames:
- U voegt het cv en de job description toe als bijlage aan de prompt.
- Er zijn geen specifieke contactgegevens of data-privacy issues in het cv die de prompt beïnvloeden.
- De letter hoeft niet in een bepaalde taal te zijn; het is Engels omdat de rol en tooling in Engels zijn.
Instellingen: effort medium.
